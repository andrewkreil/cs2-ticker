02 / ONE PRICE, FOUR OUTCOMES

Follow the cash. Count the friction.

Skinport public: 8%, or 6% for qualifying USD sales ≥ $1,000; private: 2%, requiring your own buyer. Bank/FX costs are excluded from those two rows. CSFloat: 2% sale fee, then an editable 0.5–2.5% withdrawal assumption, then FX. Fees compound; the displayed FX input is an estimate.

Steam: buyer total ÷ 1.15 approximates non-withdrawable wallet balance; minimum fees and cent rounding can differ. It is excluded from cash savings comparisons. Availability, payout holds and trade-protection reversals are separate from fees. A low fee does not ensure liquidity.

03 / FIVE-COVERT NORMALIZED WEAR BUDGET

A tiny rounding step. A different side of the cap.

Normalize each input: (float − min) ÷ (max − min). Map the mean of five normalized inputs to the selected output range.

Manual ranges are calculation scenarios.

Wear ranges do not establish recipe eligibility, output probability or acquisition availability. The light compares the projected output with your target, then applies Math.fround() to that output. It illustrates IEEE-754 binary32 storage rounding; it does not claim to reproduce unpublished game internals.
