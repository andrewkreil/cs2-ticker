Data & methodology +

Historical capacity benchmark: 7d Recap #348 (26 Oct 2026) lists $2,237.05 modeled post-fee spread, 201 alerts and 197 observed SOLD. Dividing by 40 assumed seats gives $55.93/seat/week; multiplying by 30/7 gives $239.68/seat/30d, approximately 12.0 times the $19.99 subscription fee. 201 × 30/7 ÷ 40 = 21.54 alerts/seat/30d only under hypothetical equal allocation. The lower 24h Recap #358 (27 Oct 2026) sample pace of $134.42/day gives $100.82/seat/30d, approximately 5.0 times the fee. These are dated linear extrapolations, not a funded pool, member payouts or observed monthly returns; signals are shared and trades are not assigned to seats. A single capture covers a month only if its actual net gain reaches $19.99. The 40-seat policy applies per pod; multiple pods do not create new listings. Audit counters and the Butterfly example cover 21–22 Oct 2026; channel cards retain their public UTC timestamps and the JSON records their refresh time. SOLD records listing status, with buyer and realized returns unknown. Spreads and ROI are modeled after assumed fees and before tax; Model 4 uses 0.962 recovery, not a universal platform fee. Free delivery has a 15-minute delay; VIP removes that intentional delay while processing and network time remain. The 40-seat limit is a pod capacity; current availability and billing terms are on Whop. Float calculations use the declared normalized-input model and editable example ranges; item eligibility, trade protection and payout conditions require separate checks.
