What this estimate includes +

Model 4 uses a fixed 3.8% friction assumption, not an official fee schedule. Estimated spread = benchmark × 0.962 − buy price. Maximum price = min(benchmark × 0.962 − $2.50, benchmark × 0.962 ÷ 1.06), rounded down to the nearest cent.

The label “safe” means these two model thresholds are met. It does not cover price changes, failed sales, trade reversals, taxes or guaranteed liquidity.

Read the two-step float math +

Normalize each input: (actual float − input minimum) ÷ (input maximum − input minimum). Average the five normalized values. Output float = average × (output maximum − output minimum) + output minimum.

Remaining average budget = (5 × target normalized average − sum of owned normalized inputs) ÷ remaining slots. Displayed strict upper bounds are rounded down conservatively; use a buffer. This tool does not validate collections, contract eligibility or which knife/glove you will receive.

The 0.06–0.80 preset is an editable capped-range example, not a verified inventory lookup. Check the exact finish and current game behavior before entering values. The normalized formula is the declared model used by this tool; the cited Valve update confirms contract eligibility, not this formula.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Understand the tool before you act.

What is the difference between the free feed and VIP?+

The free Telegram web preview has a 15-minute delay and can be read without logging in. The $19.99/month VIP plan is designed for 40 seats; check current availability at checkout. VIP alerts have no intentional publishing delay, but network, platform and processing latency still exist. The 55.11-second median shown here is from observed SOLD signals in the fixed 21–22 October 2026 sample, not a promise about future signals or execution. Low-float inventory monitoring is not confirmed as a product feature on this page.

Does Model 4's 0.962 equal an official marketplace fee?+

No. Multiplying a fair benchmark by 0.962 applies the declared Model 4 assumption of 3.8% resale friction. It is not a universal Skinport or CSFloat fee. The separate marketplace comparison uses its own editable seller and payout/FX inputs, applied sequentially. Estimated net spread is after those modeled costs and before tax; it is not proof of a completed profitable resale.

Can five Covert skins be traded up to a knife or gloves?+

Valve's 22 October 2025 update confirms that five StatTrak Covert inputs can produce a StatTrak knife, while five regular Covert inputs can produce a regular knife or gloves, from eligible collections represented by the inputs. This calculator uses a declared two-step normalized-input float model; the cited Valve notes do not establish that formula. Check current game behavior, exact finish ranges and input eligibility separately. A float budget does not predict the output item or its probability.

Do I need a Steam login, API key or browser extension?+

No. You do not need to connect Steam, provide an API key or install an extension. Calculator inputs are processed locally in your browser and are not submitted by this page. The page loads a public telemetry JSON snapshot to refresh the evidence panel. It does not buy or sell items, control your inventory or promise that an alert will remain available.

How do trade protection and withdrawal delays affect cashout?+

Steam describes a 7-day trade-protection period for protected CS2 items received in a trade, during which eligible trades may be reversed. CSFloat terms describe associated funds being held for approximately 8 days. Custodial marketplaces reduce direct counterparty cancellation exposure, but custody is not a guarantee against every reversal or operational risk. Bank withdrawals are not guaranteed to arrive immediately; banking, currency and account conditions matter. The calculators do not model the opportunity cost of frozen funds, and a price gap does not establish why a seller set a price.

Trade protection: Steam official guidance · CSFloat terms. Fee and contract sources are listed above.
